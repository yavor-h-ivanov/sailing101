# Photos wanted

Photos the site still needs, in priority order. Three searches of Wikimedia Commons, Flickr and Openverse
(September 2026) found nothing usable for these. If you can supply any, they go straight in.

## How to hand them over

1. Name each file by its ID below (for example `F1.jpg`, `H1-a.jpg` for a second option). Any common
   format is fine: JPEG, HEIC, PNG or WebP, straight off a phone or camera.
2. Put them in `assets/img/incoming/` on the branch (GitHub's "Add file → Upload files" works), or attach
   them in the chat.
3. For each photo, tell me:
   - **Whose photo it is** and the name to show in the credit (for example "Photo: J. Smith").
   - **The licence.** For your own photos, say "CC BY 4.0" (anyone may reuse it with credit; recommended)
     or "CC0" (no credit needed). For someone else's photo, their licence and a link to where it is
     published. The site only uses CC BY, CC BY-SA or public-domain images.
   - For the boat photos, **the boat's model and year** if you know it.

What I do with them: resize to 1,280 pixels on the long side (1,024 for the small inline photos), save as
JPEG at about 80 % quality, and strip all metadata, including any GPS location, before anything is
committed. Then I write the caption and credit, place the photo after the matching card, update
`assets/img/LICENCES.md`, and delete the original from `incoming/`.

Good photos for this site are sharp, well lit, taken close enough that the fault fills most of the frame,
and free of faces and boat names or sail numbers you would rather not publish. A finger pointing at the
fault helps.
